Business rules & integrity constraints

RuleHow it is enforced
Seat belongs to the show's screenbooking_seat stores (booking_id, showtime_id, screen_id, seat_id) with composite FKs to booking(booking_id, showtime_id), showtime(showtime_id, screen_id) and seat(seat_id, screen_id). A seat from another screen cannot be booked.
No double booking, history keptbooking_seat.active_slot is 1 for HELD/CONFIRMED rows and NULL otherwise; UNIQUE(showtime_id, seat_id, active_slot) allows one active reservation per seat per show, while cancelled rows stay for history and the seat can be re-booked.
Seat holdsSeats are HELD (hold_expires_at) while the customer pays. PENDING bookings past payment_deadline are expired by sp_expire_unpaid_bookings() and their seats released.
No overlapping showsTriggers on showtime reject any non-cancelled show that overlaps another show on the same screen.
One ticket per seatticket.booking_seat_id is FK + UNIQUE and ticket_code is UNIQUE (used in the QR code). Tickets are issued only after a SUCCESS payment and scanned at the door (checked_in_at / checked_in_by_user_id).
Historical pricesbooking_seat.seat_price and order_detail.unit_price are snapshots; showtime_price holds the price per seat category for each show; food_item.current_price is only today's menu price.
Web and counter salesbooking_channel is WEB or COUNTER. Walk-in customers have user_id NULL with guest_phone; counter sales record sold_by_user_id.
Payments and refundspayment.payment_type is PAYMENT or REFUND. A booking can have several attempts; only SUCCESS rows count. Net revenue = SUCCESS payments - SUCCESS refunds.
PromotionsA booking may use at most one promotion (booking.promotion_id) and stores the discount_amount; validity dates and usage limits are checked by the application inside the booking transaction.
Rolesuser_account.role (CUSTOMER, ADMIN, MANAGER, BOX_OFFICE) replaces separate customer/staff tables; staff are linked to a branch via cinema_id (CHECK: customers have none).
Auditaudit_log records who changed what, with old and new values.
Derived values not storedScreen capacity = COUNT of active seats. order_detail.line_total is a generated column (quantity x unit_price).
